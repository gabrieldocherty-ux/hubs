BluModel Studio: 3D kitchen design showrooms for kitchen and bath companies

BluModel Studio (often searched as BluModel or Blue Model) builds, hosts and runs your own 3D kitchen and bath design website, under your company's name and with your products. Homeowners plan their kitchen or bathroom online, in plan and in 3D, on a phone or a computer, and every design arrives in your inbox as a lead.

A virtual kitchen showroom, built for you

An online kitchen planner and kitchen visualizer that is white-label: your logo, your colours, your cabinet lines, appliances and finishes, on your own web address. Customers draw their room, place your cabinets, see it in 3D and send it to you for a quote. No software to install and no designer time to set up.

Who it's for

Kitchen and bath showrooms, cabinet makers and dealers, renovation and design-build companies, and home builders, across Ontario, Alberta and the rest of Canada. Kitchen design software for cabinet showrooms that doubles as kitchen design lead generation.

What it costs

$50 a month in Canadian dollars for hosting and running your showroom at your own web address, with updates and daily backups. Plus a one-time setup quoted for your company.
