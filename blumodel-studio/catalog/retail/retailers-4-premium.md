# Retail research, round 4: premium appliances (checked 2026-10-19)

`retailers-4-premium.json` adds 30 premium appliances that showroom designers specify, from Best Buy and
Home Depot Canada: Bosch (300 and 800 Series dishwashers, 800 Series counter-depth fridges, 36 in. induction,
gas and dual fuel ranges), Miele (G 5006 and G 5216 dishwashers), Café (matte white counter-depth and 4-door
fridges, 30 in. slide-in dual fuel, induction and gas ranges, commercial and wall hoods, single and double
wall ovens, CustomFit dishwasher) and KitchenAid (36 in. induction and dual fuel ranges, 36 in. canopy hood).

Read from search results only (no page fetched), confidence `medium`. Prices are snapshots and listings
often disagree; treat them as indicative. Finishes of the same model merge into one product.

## Not found at stores the app links to

- **Fisher & Paykel**: Home Depot Canada only carries parts; Best Buy search returned no listings.
- **JennAir**: no Best Buy or Home Depot Canada listings surfaced.
- **Sub-Zero, Wolf, Thermador, Monogram**: sold through dealers, not the stores the catalog links to.
  Adding them would need either dealer links (a new store type) or the brand's own spec sheets.
